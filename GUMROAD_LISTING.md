## Name

Kitchlore - Recipe Keeper for Android

## Summary (short line)

Your recipes, scanned, imported, scaled, and cooked - all on your phone, no account required.

## Description

Kitchlore is a recipe keeper for Android that keeps everything on your device. No account, no cloud, no subscription.

- Scan recipes - photograph a cookbook page or handwritten card and Kitchlore reads it, on-device, with amounts and steps you can review and edit before saving
- Import from the web - paste a link, or share a recipe page from your browser straight into Kitchlore
- Recipe hunt - search by dish or by appliance, like "chicken dinner in the Ninja Foodi", and get recipe options you can bring straight into your library
- Pantry mode - tell it what you have, and see recipes you can cook right now ranked by what's missing
- Cook mode - full-screen steps, ingredients alongside, built to follow with messy hands
- Servings scaler - every quantity scales, fractions, mixed numbers and whole numbers alike
- Photo category grid, ingredient search, saved recipes, light and dark themes with seasonal palettes

Your library never leaves your phone, and everyday features work offline. The handwriting reader is one exception: the first time you scan, the app downloads a small AI model, a one-time download best done over Wi-Fi.
