# Kitchlore privacy policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Kitchlore is an offline-first recipe keeper for Android. It was built to keep your cooking life on your own device.

## What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. Kitchlore has no accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. Your recipes, photos and library are never uploaded anywhere.

Two things do travel over the network, and only when you ask the app to: the search words you type into recipe search, sent to public search endpoints, and the recipe pages you choose to import, fetched from the sites that host them.

## What stays on your device

Recipes, photos, pantry items, tags, settings, and your library all live in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app removes everything, permanently.

## Camera and photos

Photos you capture inside the app are saved only to the app's own private storage. They are never written to your gallery.

## Importing and search

Importing a recipe fetches the recipe page directly from the site it lives on. Keyless recipe search sends your search words to public search endpoints so the app can find recipe pages, and stores nothing about you. The small AI model that helps read handwritten recipes and tidy measurements runs entirely on your device; nothing is sent to a server for processing.
